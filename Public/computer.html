Get a smoother connection

Remote access can use a lot of CPU/GPU power on the sharing PC and in this browser, especially at high FPS. Keep browser graphics acceleration enabled where available, close heavy tabs/apps, and keep laptops plugged in.

Use a stable wired or strong Wi-Fi connection. Start with 60 FPS and Balanced quality in the launcher; use Fast quality if video stutters. Higher settings can increase load and delay. Actual performance depends on both devices and the network.
