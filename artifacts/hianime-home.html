About Hianime: anime online, free and in HD

Hianime is a free anime site where the whole catalogue is one search away. Type a name in English or Japanese, or browse by type, year, season and score, and every result opens a page with the story, the studio, the season it aired and the full episode list.

Series, movies and everything between

The library holds 8,028 anime series and 1,343 anime movies, plus OVAs, ONAs and specials. Shows still airing sit next to finished classics, so a new season and its first season are never far apart.

New episodes and the charts

The home page is refreshed every day with new anime episodes, the top airing anime and the most popular anime of all time. Shows that are on the way wait in coming soon.

Hianime, Hi Anime: one site

You will see the name written as Hianime, Hi Anime. They all lead to the same library, the same My Anime list and the same players, so bookmark hianime.ms and you will always land in the right place.

Questions people ask

What is Hianime?

Hianime is a free place to watch anime in your browser. The library holds 9,371 titles, from long running shonen to one season romances and feature films, and each page tells you up front whether there is subtitled audio, an English dub, or both. Open the home page to see what is new today.

Do I have to pay or make an account?

No. There is no price and no sign-up. You can press play right away. If you want to keep a list, My Anime starts by itself the first time you save a show, without an email or a password.

Can I pick between sub and dub?

Yes. The green CC badge counts subtitled episodes and the blue mic badge counts dubbed ones. On the player page you switch between the two with one tap whenever a dub exists. The movies and TV series lists show both badges on every poster, and the Language filter on the browse page shows only anime with an English dub.

How do I keep track of what I am watching?

Use My Anime. Save a show with the plus button, and Hianime remembers the last episode you played, ticks the ones you finished and can tell you when a new episode of a show you follow comes out. A private key lets you open the same list on another phone or computer.

When do new episodes appear?

Episodes are added as they come out. The airing schedule lists what airs each day in Japan time for the next week, and the New episodes list on the home page shows the latest arrivals.

Is Hi Anime the same site as Hianime?

Yes. Hianime, Hi Anime and hianime all mean this site. Whichever spelling you type, you land on the same library, the same My Anime list and the same players. Bookmark https://hianime.ms so you always have the right address.

A video does not start. What should I do?

Every episode has several players. Pick another server name under the video, or switch between sub and dub. If none of them work, press Report under the player and tell us which episode it is, so we can look at it.

Can I watch on my phone or my TV?

Yes. The site works in any up to date browser on phones, tablets, laptops and smart TVs, and the layout adapts to the screen. There is no app to install: on a phone you can add Hianime to your home screen from the browser menu.
