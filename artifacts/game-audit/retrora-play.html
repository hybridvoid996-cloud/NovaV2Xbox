Controls

Keyboard and mouse

WASD move, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Space jump / leave the bus / glider
Left click fire, right click aim, R reload, E open or pick up
1-5 or the wheel weapons, F pickaxe, M map, Esc menu
Q build mode, F1 wall, F2 floor, F3 stairs, F4 roof, left click place, right click material, R turn
F8 admin panel in Offline Mode or as an online admin
G edit the piece you are aiming at, drag over cells on the piece to select, Enter confirm, 0 reset, Backspace cancel
Hit trees, rocks and cars with the pickaxe for wood, stone and metal

Controller

Left stick move (click to sprint), right stick look
RT fire (or build), LT aim (or switch material), A jump / leave the bus / glider, tap B build mode, hold B edit, click R3 to toggle crouch
X reload (hold to open or pick up), Y pickaxe, LB / RB weapons
Start menu, View map; in menus the d-pad moves, A selects, B goes back
While editing: right stick aims, RT selects cells on the piece, A confirms, Y or LT resets, B cancels
Touch: drag the left side to move and the right side to look. Crouch and Sprint toggle; opening a menu or switching apps releases all touch controls.
