Workforce

Apply for CHW Training

Our 16-week Community Health Worker Training Academy prepares Baltimore residents to serve as certified frontline health navigators. The program is fully funded through grants, with no tuition cost to participants. Priority is given to residents of medically underserved ZIP codes who demonstrate strong community ties and a commitment to health equity. Graduates earn Maryland DHMH CHW certification and receive individualized career placement support, with a 94% job placement rate within 90 days of graduation.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Baltimore City or Baltimore County resident (age 18 or older)
  • High school diploma or GED equivalent
  • Ability to commit to 16-week full-time schedule (Mon-Thu, 9am-3pm)
  • Demonstrated community involvement or health advocacy experience
  • Bilingual proficiency preferred but not required

Next Cohort: Spring 2027 — Applications open through March 1

Research

Volunteer as a Data Collector

Our Neighborhood Health Needs Assessment program recruits and trains community data collectors for each assessment cycle. As a community data collector, you will receive 40 hours of paid training in survey administration, informed consent procedures, and community engagement methods, then conduct door-to-door household health surveys in your neighborhood alongside experienced field coordinators. Data collectors are compensated at $18 per hour for all training and fieldwork hours.

What You Will Learn

  • Community health research methods and ethics
  • Survey administration and data quality protocols
  • Informed consent and respondent confidentiality
  • Community engagement and motivational interviewing basics
  • GIS mapping and health resource inventory techniques

Next assessment cycle: Summer 2027 (neighborhoods TBA)

Partnership

Institutional Partnership

CC Health Development partners with Federally Qualified Health Centers, hospital systems, health departments, academic institutions, and community-based organizations to expand the reach and impact of our programs. Partnership models include CHW practicum site hosting, joint research collaborations, clinic capacity building engagements, mobile clinic route coordination, and co-investment in shared health equity data infrastructure.

Current Partner Categories

  • Health systems and FQHCs seeking CHW integration support
  • Academic institutions for research and pipeline programs
  • Government agencies for assessment and planning collaboration
  • Community organizations for grassroots health promotion
  • Technology partners for health data infrastructure
Support

Donate to CC Health Dev

As a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization (EIN: 52-3018472), all contributions to CC Health Development are fully tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Your investment directly supports Community Health Worker training scholarships, neighborhood health assessment field operations, mobile clinic fuel and medical supply costs, and youth public health pipeline program mentorship activities.

Where Your Investment Goes

  • $500 sponsors one CHW trainee's clinical practicum materials
  • $1,500 funds a community data collector for one assessment cycle
  • $5,000 supports one mobile clinic service day (including staffing)
  • $10,000 funds a full Youth Pipeline summer cohort participant
